# Site Saarthi

Site Saarthi is a role-based construction management platform designed to bring real-time visibility, accountability, and efficiency to construction projects.

## Overview

India’s construction industry is one of the largest in the world, yet site operations are still managed through fragmented systems such as paper registers, phone calls, and messaging applications. This results in inefficiencies, lack of transparency, and significant financial losses.

Site Saarthi addresses this gap by providing a unified digital platform that connects all stakeholders and streamlines on-site operations.

## Problem

Construction projects consistently suffer due to:

- Lack of real-time visibility for decision-makers  
- Manual and unreliable tracking of attendance and progress  
- Poor coordination between multiple stakeholders  
- Inefficient material management and approvals  
- High financial leakage due to delays, wastage, and rework  

These issues are structural and exist across projects of all scales.

## Solution

Site Saarthi replaces fragmented workflows with a centralized, role-based system that ensures accurate data flow across all levels of a construction project.

- Owners gain real-time project visibility  
- Engineers log structured, GPS-tagged progress reports  
- Workers mark location-based attendance  
- Materials are tracked from request to consumption  

The platform is designed specifically for real-world construction environments, including low-connectivity sites.

## Key Features

### Daily Progress Reports (DPR)
- Structured reporting with photo evidence  
- Automated report generation  
- Historical tracking for audits and analysis  

### GPS-Based Attendance
- Location-verified check-ins  
- Eliminates proxy attendance  
- Improves workforce accountability  

### Material Tracking & Ordering
- Monitor inventory and consumption  
- Approval-based material requests  
- Reduces wastage and delays  

### Centralized Communication
- Single platform for all stakeholders  
- Reduces dependency on informal communication channels  

### AI Insights
- Identifies inefficiencies and delays  
- Provides actionable recommendations  
- Supports data-driven decision making  

### Owner Dashboard
- Multi-site monitoring  
- High-level analytics and reports  

### Offline & Low Bandwidth Support
- Designed for real construction environments  
- Syncs data when connectivity is restored  

## User Roles
 
- **Engineer** – DPR updates and site-level management  
- **Developer** – Approvals, Operational oversight and decision-making  
- **Client** – Real-time monitoring

## Impact

Site Saarthi directly targets the three largest sources of loss in construction:

- Labour inefficiency  
- Material wastage  
- Coordination delays  

By digitizing and structuring workflows, the platform enables:

- Improved operational efficiency  
- Reduced project delays  
- Better financial control  
- Increased transparency and accountability  

Even a marginal improvement in efficiency can translate into significant cost savings at scale.

## System Design

- Frontend: Flutter (Mobile-first approach)  
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js, Kafka, Socket.io (API-driven architecture)  
- Database: MongoDB, Hive 
- Architecture: Offline-first with sync capabilities  
- Integration: GPS and analytics modules
